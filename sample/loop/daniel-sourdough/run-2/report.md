# Raport: Daniel, 45 — przebieg 2

## Jak poszło
I got the sourdough saved with Levain, Dough and Method as separate headed sections, and every gram came out exactly as I typed it, which is what mattered most to me. The A+ button made the text big enough to read from the bread bin, and marking the loaf "to try" was easy; it then showed up alone under the To try filter. I couldn't properly check that the screen stays awake, since that needs a long wait, so I can't vouch for it yet. Overall I'm pleased and would keep using it for my baking notes, with a few small niggles.

## Problemy
- **[drobny]** The method steps show times ("1 hour", "30 min", "4-5 h", "12-14 h") as orange timer chips, and these break my sentences across lines. A stray full stop or bracket ends up alone on its own line, e.g. "for [1 hour] ." and "( [12-14 h] )". The content is intact, but it looks untidy, and I didn't ask for the chips. [shot-014](shots/shot-014.png) [shot-013](shots/shot-013.png)
- **[drobny]** In the recipe view the first look at step 1 seemed to show a strikethrough and a garbled "1 hour". For a moment I thought my text had been mangled. It turned out to be the gesture bar overlapping the text and the chip rendering, but it gave me a scare, and I'm touchy about my grams. [shot-012](shots/shot-012.png)
- **[drobny]** The "to try" group isn't a section on the home screen. I had to go into "All recipes" and find a filter chip, and the keyboard popped up on its own and covered the list. It works, but it wasn't obvious where my untried recipes would live. [shot-020](shots/shot-020.png) [shot-021](shots/shot-021.png)
- **[drobny]** The new recipe form puts the keyboard over half the screen, so the Ingredients and Instructions boxes are barely visible and I had to scroll before each one. Typing a long recipe like that is fiddly. [shot-004](shots/shot-004.png) [shot-007](shots/shot-007.png)

## Życzenia i inspiracje
- **Let me switch off the timer chips**, or at least keep them inline with the sentence so the text reads as I wrote it. [shot-014](shots/shot-014.png)
- **A bigger maximum text size**, or one tap to a "kitchen mode" size. Even after several A+ taps I still wanted it a bit larger from a metre away. [shot-016](shots/shot-016.png) [shot-017](shots/shot-017.png)
- **A "to try" section on the home screen**, next to the cookbooks, so I don't have to dig through a filter. [shot-020](shots/shot-020.png)
- **A scale button (x2, x1.5) for the ingredients.** I double recipes for parties and the app could do the maths on the grams. [shot-011](shots/shot-011.png)
- **A visible "screen stays awake" indicator** in the recipe view, so I know it's on while my hands are covered in dough. [shot-011](shots/shot-011.png)

## Uwagi końcowe
The important thing works: the formatting and the grams survived untouched. If the timer chips become optional, I'll happily move all my baking notes over.
