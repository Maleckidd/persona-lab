## Przepis: Auntie Carol's Trifle

Cała treść ekranu przepisu, od góry:

    A+
    Auntie Carol's Trifle
    INGREDIENTS
    1 packet trifle sponges (8)
    3 tbsp sherry
    400 g frozen raspbe thawed
    1 packet raspberry jel
    500 ml ready-ma custar
    300 ml double cream
    1 tbsp icing sugar
    Flaked almonds to finish
    Add to shopping list
    INSTRUCTIONS
    Break sponges into a glass bowl and drizzle with sherry. Scatter raspberries over. Make up the jelly with 500 ml water, pour over and chill until set (about ￼). Spread custard on top. Whip cream with icing sugar to soft peaks, spoon over, sprinkle with almonds. Chill until serving.
