## Przepis: Spaghetti Carbonara

    SERVINGS
    2
    INGREDIENTS
    200 g spaghetti
    100 g pancetta or smoked bacon
    2 eggs + 2 egg yolks
    50 g pecorino romano, finely grated
    50 g parmesan, finely grated
    Salt and freshly ground black pepper

    INSTRUCTIONS
    1.
    Cook spaghetti al dente in heavily salted water.
    2.
    Fry pancetta in a dry pan until golden, about ￼. Remove from heat.
    5 min
    3.
    In a bowl, whisk together eggs, yolks, and grated cheese. Season generously with pepper.
    4.
    Reserve a cup of pasta cooking water, then drain the pasta.
    5.
    Toss hot pasta into the pan with pancetta (heat off). Add 3 tbsp of cooking water.
    6.
    Add the egg mixture and stir vigorously — the sauce should be creamy, not scrambled.
    7.
    Add more cooking water if needed. Serve immediately.
    NOTES
    The key is residual heat only — never put the pan back on the stove or the eggs will scramble.

## Przepis: Classic Tiramisu

Cała treść ekranu przepisu, od góry:

    A+
    Classic Tiramisu
    PREP
    30 min
    TOTAL
    30 min
    SERVINGS
    6
    INGREDIENTS
    500 g mascarpone
    4 eggs, separated
    100 g icing sugar
    200 ml strong espresso, cooled
    3 tbsp amaretto or rum
    200 g savoiardi ladyfinger biscuits
    Cocoa powder for dusting
    Add to shopping list
    INSTRUCTIONS
    1.
    Beat egg yolks with icing sugar until pale and fluffy.
    2.
    Add mascarpone and mix until smooth.
    3.
    Beat egg whites with a pinch of salt to stiff peaks. Gently fold into the mascarpone mixture.
    4.
    Combine espresso and alcohol in a shallow dish.
    5.
    Briefly dip each ladyfinger in the coffee mixture (￼ per side — don’t over-soak).
    1–2 seconds
    6.
    Arrange a layer of biscuits in a dish, spread half the cream on top.
    7.
    Repeat with remaining biscuits and cream.
    8.
    Refrigerate for at least ￼, preferably overnight.
    4 hours
    9.
    Dust generously with sifted cocoa powder before serving.
    NOTES
    It tastes even better the next day — the flavours meld beautifully overnight.
