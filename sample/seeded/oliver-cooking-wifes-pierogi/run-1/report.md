# Raport: Oliver, 52 — przebieg 1

## Jak poszło
I did not manage either of my needs. I never found Beata's pierogi recipe, so I never got as far as reading it or changing the flour from 500 g to 600 g. I searched, then scrolled through the list, and in the end gave up and decided to ring her. I feel let down: I thought the app was meant to keep her recipe safe, and I would not rely on it in the middle of cooking.

## Problemy
- **[krytyczny]** I typed "pierogi" in the search box and the list went empty with the message "No recipes yet. Add your first one!" [shot-003](shots/shot-003.png). That is a baffling thing to say when I have been using the app for weeks and recipes were showing a moment before. I thought my recipes had gone, and I did not know whether the recipe was missing or the search was broken.
- **[poważny]** Pressing the magnifying glass on the keyboard changed nothing [shot-004](shots/shot-004.png). I had no idea whether it was working or whether the recipe simply was not there.
- **[poważny]** I scrolled through the whole list and found only Italian dishes and pies, with no pierogi [shot-005](shots/shot-005.png) [shot-008](shots/shot-008.png) [shot-009](shots/shot-009.png). I could not tell whether the recipe was never saved, was in another cookbook, or was hidden. The app gave me no hint, so I had no way to work it out myself.
- **[drobny]** The recipe tiles are huge grey pictures, so only about three fit on screen and a short list takes a lot of scrolling [shot-007](shots/shot-007.png) [shot-008](shots/shot-008.png). With floury hands, that is tiring.

## Życzenia i inspiracje
- **A clearer message when a search finds nothing**, such as "Nothing found for 'pierogi'", instead of "No recipes yet" [shot-003](shots/shot-003.png).
- **Smaller tiles, or a compact list option**, so I can see more recipes at once and scroll less [shot-007](shots/shot-007.png).
- **A hint about where recipes might be**, such as a note that search covers every cookbook, or a way to see which cookbook each recipe is in [shot-001](shots/shot-001.png).

## Uwagi końcowe
I only wanted to read one recipe and fix one number. If it had been easy to find, I would have been happy. As it was, ringing my wife was quicker.
