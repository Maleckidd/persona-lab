# Raport: Emily, 29 — przebieg 1

## Jak poszło
I didn't get what I came for. The link import for the brown butter cookies failed on the first try, and the text-paste fallback only filled in the title, with the ingredients box looking empty. I never got as far as finding the "to try" option, so my second need went untested. It took 10 turns and gave me nothing usable. As it stands it's no better than a screenshot or a browser bookmark, so I'm uninstalling tonight.

## Problemy
- **[krytyczny]** The link import, the main reason I installed this, failed with a vague red error: "Couldn't load that page. Check the link and your connection." It didn't say whether the problem was the link, the site or my connection. I had no way to tell what to fix, and one failure is all I give a recipe app. [shot-006](shots/shot-006.png)
- **[krytyczny]** The paste-text fallback only brought the title into the form. The ingredients box still showed grey placeholder text and looked empty, and prep, cook and servings were empty too. I'd have to fill it in by hand, which is exactly what I installed the app to avoid. [shot-010](shots/shot-010.png)
- **[poważny]** The import is hidden behind a button called "More" with a download icon on a blank manual form. When I tapped the plus I expected a paste-a-link option straight away. The name gave me no hint that import lives there. [shot-002](shots/shot-002.png)
- **[drobny]** Nothing was auto-filled from my clipboard in the URL field, so I had to type the whole link myself. For a paste-a-link flow that feels like a miss. [shot-003](shots/shot-003.png) [shot-004](shots/shot-004.png)
- **[drobny]** After I typed the link, the field only showed the start of it ("https://www.sallysbaki"), so I couldn't tell whether the full link was there. I couldn't rule out that the failure came from a truncated link. [shot-006](shots/shot-006.png)
- **[drobny]** A "Breakfasts" cookbook was already in the app although I hadn't added anything. It's odd on a first launch, though it didn't block me. [shot-001](shots/shot-001.png)

## Życzenia i inspiracje
- **Make link import work first time and show a specific error.** If it fails, say why (no connection, site blocked, no recipe found) and offer a quick fallback such as "paste the page text instead". [shot-006](shots/shot-006.png)
- **Put the import option on the first screen after tapping plus.** A clear "Paste link" choice, ideally pre-filled from the clipboard, would fit what a paste-a-link app promises. [shot-002](shots/shot-002.png) [shot-003](shots/shot-003.png)
- **When importing pasted text, split it into ingredients and steps automatically**, and show it filled in so I can quickly check it. [shot-010](shots/shot-010.png)
- **Show the "to try" choice on the form where I can see it**, so I'm not hunting for it. I never found it. [shot-010](shots/shot-010.png)

## Uwagi końcowe
The "From link" tab being preselected was a nice touch, and I liked that the Import button only lit up once there was input. But the core job of getting a clean recipe in failed twice, and I won't spend more time on it.
